Autonomous Disaster Digital Twin — Connected Architecture
System Design Specification • Open-Source Integration Matrix

1. Computer Vision Layer

The perception engine consumes live video feeds (WebCam or Drone Streams). It executes color segmentation, Gaussian edge filters, and contour tracking to estimate flood water boundaries and detect debris obstructions on road corridors in real time.

2. XGBoost Vulnerability Model

Built on XGBoost regression, the risk model scores individual buildings based on effective elevation, proximity to ocean water, and structural height. Inference takes less than 2 milliseconds per building without GPU overhead.

3. A* Dynamic Evacuation Engine

The city road network is represented as an edge-weighted graph. Path cost prioritizes safety over distance: flooded or blocked roads receive infinite weight. When an active route is severed, the engine executes dynamic replanning in under 5 milliseconds.

4. Voice Emergency Dispatcher

Uses the HTML5 Speech Synthesis API to verbally broadcast turn-by-turn evacuation instructions. Citizens and decision-makers hear the exact corridor to follow, distance, and safe haven elevation.
